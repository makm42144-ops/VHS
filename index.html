How we operate

Licensed supply

Verification

Trade accounts.

Wholesaler
Your wholesaler licence number and the issuing state or territory authority.
Research institution
Institutional name, the ethics approval reference for the work, and the chief investigator’s contact details.

Once your account is open, ordering runs as a purchase order against it. Accounts are re-verified annually and revoked immediately if a registration lapses.
