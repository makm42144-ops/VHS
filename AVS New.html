04 / 05

What we check, and against what

Trade accounts.

A number typed into a form is an assertion, so we check it against the register that issued it before an account is opened. Applications are verified within seconds.

Community and hospital pharmacy
Pharmacy Board of Australia approval number, and the name of the pharmacist in charge.
Wholesaler
Your wholesaler licence number and the issuing state or territory authority.
Research institution
Institutional name, the ethics approval reference for the work, and the chief investigator’s contact details.

Once your account is open, ordering runs as a purchase order against it. Accounts are re-verified annually and revoked immediately if a registration lapses. Access and orders are logged, which is a record-keeping requirement under state and territory poisons legislation rather than a preference.
